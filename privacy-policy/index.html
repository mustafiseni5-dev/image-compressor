Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy describes how Utilora approaches information when you use this website. Review it together with the privacy information provided by your browser, hosting provider, and any third-party services that may appear on the site.

Information you enter into tools

Core image tools are designed to process selected images in your browser rather than uploading them to a Utilora image-processing server. Text and calculation tools generally work in the browser. Do not assume every browser extension, network service, or third-party script is local; avoid using sensitive information in a general-purpose tool unless you have verified the full workflow.

Technical information and hosting

When a website is loaded, the hosting platform and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and requested resources for delivery, security, and diagnostics. The exact logs retained depend on the hosting provider and its configuration.

Advertising and cookies

Utilora may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and process information according to user consent, applicable law, and their own policies. If personalized ads are served to users in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, the site owner must configure a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, as required by Google’s policies. You can learn more in Google’s advertising information and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Choices and consent

Depending on your location, you may be shown choices about advertising cookies and personalized ads. You can revisit consent settings through the consent message or the site’s available privacy controls, where provided. Browser settings can also help manage cookies, though blocking them may affect site functionality.

Children

This site is not intended to solicit personal information from children. Please contact us if you believe personal information has been submitted inappropriately.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the site’s features, providers, or legal obligations change. The date above indicates the most recent stated update.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@utilora.fun.
