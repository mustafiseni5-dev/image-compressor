Average Calculator

Find the arithmetic mean of a set of numbers. This page explains when to use it, how to interpret its output, and what to check before relying on the result.

Enter a list of numbers to calculate their arithmetic mean. Add the values together and divide by the number of valid values; the result gives one summary of the set.

What to know before using it

The mean can be affected strongly by outliers. For skewed data, the median may better represent a typical value. Make sure your input uses the expected decimal and separator format.

Practical tip

Check whether blanks, missing values, or outliers should be excluded before interpreting the result.
